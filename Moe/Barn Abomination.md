The Barn Abomination is an amalgam monster that Moe encounters and destroys during the events of [[Ridgewreath Farm]]. Like the [[Lost Souls]], the Barn Abomination is the result of the [[Annemarie McCall|Anna's]] pursuit of [[Soul Transfer]]. Unlike the Lost Souls, the remaining bodies of those whose souls were ripped from them began to lose shape and will in the presence of the magic of the area. As a result, they melded into a massive creature that exists inside of the barn that Moe goes to find a ladder.
