Annemarie is a character in the series. She is the primary antagonist of [[The Farm (Project)||The Farm]] and may remain a supporting character throughout the series, depending on logistics.

Annemarie is a very powerful spellcaster with a natural ability to tap into her considerable well of magical energy. Throughout her whole life, she used her magical abilities without realizing it, consequently creating an intense concentration of energy at [[Ridgewreath Farm]] that is the source of the strange occurrences that happen there.

Annemarie comes from a rough, neglectful upbringing. As teenagers, Annemarie met Waylon. When she found out she was pregnant with his child, they fled, fearing the wrath of her parents. They found shelter with Waylon's aunt and uncle, who would eventually leave their farm to the young couple in their will when they passed years later.  **DEV/NARRATIVE NOTE:** It is important to show that this family is supportive, and that Waylon and Annemarie really love each other. 

As Waylon worked to keep the farm, Annemarie developed a passion for photography after finding a camera in the attic. She learned that she had an uncanny ability to capture real life through the camera, which unbeknownst to her became her spellcasting tool. She captured many laconic, beautiful scenes - winds blowing across the field, turning fields into waves; a beautiful sunny day, illuminating the man and child she loved dearly. She turned this into a small business of her own, able to capture the best moments of the people and world around her, a reflection of the joy she was denied as a child. The couple use their proceedings to turn the farm into a bed and breakfast, and enjoy some years of success doing so.

On a seemingly innocuous day, Annemarie's child - while out playing with the boat his father made for him - lost control of the toy as it traveled along the creek. Desperate to get it back, the child falls into the creek and drowns. Annemarie falls into a deep depression and stops taking pictures - and as a result, as though she had been the one thing holding it all back, the farm fell on extremely hard times. Their crops begin to wither, their animals die, and - notably - they are besieged by a tornado that destroys the grave of their child.

One day, [[Priest(Narrative)|a warlock in disguise as a traveling priest]] came to see Annemarie and Waylon after he, in his words, "felt [her] sadness for miles." He came in to hear the couples' woes. He used this time to show that Annemarie was an extremely capable, if undisciplined, magician, and gave her some guidance and tools to use her powers. He reframed the tornado as a testament to her magical abilities, a wild force of nature, and she could harness it for other purposes. She could get back what she had. 

Specifically, he was able to convince her that she capable of harnessing souls from the world around her, implying that she would be able to reclaim her child in due time. He instructed Annemarie that she would be able to bring her son back by finding him an appropriate host body, as his current physical state was far too decayed to be effective. 

Unbeknownst to the couple - while it was true that Annemarie was a powerful magician, her abilities in that moment had been enhanced and manipulated by the warlock in real time. In truth, this sort of magic is just not possible for most people to understand (and, thus, use). Even if Annemarie *could* do it, the priest had deceived her. He instead set her on a path to consistently make a well of dark, miserable energy from which he could personally draw power, with Annemarie acting as a battery for him.

Annemarie and Waylon would work together to "save" their child by compelling people to the farm, and then attempting a "soul transfer" on the people she found. The tragedy is that Annemarie did not know enough to know what she was doing - she not only *wasn't* bringing her son back, but she was also condemning innocent people to an eternity of wandering the magical prison of her farmstead while their bodies rotted, changed, and mutated. The priests' lies extended to the fact that Annemarie could return the souls to the bodies she claimed them from - and, thus, no one would ever really be hurt.

Convinced by the priest that she could save them all (including her son), she maintained this practice for many years until the power she had seeped out beyond the farm itself, creating invisible threads that compel people and animals toward the farm. These threads are the instigating factors of [[Cassie]] seeking out [[Moe]], which in turn led her to Ridgewreath Farm. This also attracted the attention of the [[Osenists]], who will begin investigating the Farm near the middle of the game. However, Annemarie will kill the operating agents, ending their initial investigation and muddying their understanding of the events of the first game.

*Gameplay*
As Moe/the player progress the story, visages of Annemarie, Waylon, and their history on Ridgewreath Farm will be seen through investigation. At first, these visions are passive.

However, as Moe makes further progress on the case, Annemarie will become aware of her interference and begin interacting with her. She will mention that the priest warned her about Moe and will be actively aggressive toward her around the midway mark of the game.

Annemarie will be a major encounter at least twice in the game. In her first encounter with Moe, she will display considerable magical power that is impressive and frightening in how undisciplined it is. Moe's task will be to escape Annemarie's assault so she can find a way to control the woman's powers.

The second encounter with Annemarie - and the final encounter of the game - sees Annemarie engaged with Moe and [[Midnight]] in a duel that spans a sizeable portion of Ridgewreath Farm. It will begin at the site of the [[Soul Transfer]] she is attempting. She will be distracted by either Scott or Midnight, allowing Moe time to free [[Emilio]]. This will enrage Annemarie, and she will be overcome with the powers she is wielding, making her extremely dangerous. Moe will lead her to the main farmhouse so Emilio and Midnight can escape.

The conflict can end in one of two ways, depending on how the player handles the fight. The [[Ending 1 (The Farm)|first ending]] sees Moe weakening Annemarie enough to actually kill her. As the power is released from her body and Ridgewreath Farm itself, the house will collapse on top of Moe. Emilio and Midnight will come to free Moe from the rubble of the house, and in the epilogue, Moe will lament that she couldn't do more for Annemarie, but despite all that she has done, she never deserved to suffer for as long as she did.

The [[True Ending (The Farm)|second ending]] sees Moe leading Annemarie deeper into the house, into the secret area resembling her child's room. This option will be harder and requires a more defensive approach from the player, as Moe will need to protect herself across all 3 stories of the house. However, once Moe makes it to the room, Annemarie will become inconsolable with grief and break down at the foot of her child's bed.

Annemarie's appearance in future games will depend on how the first game is made. If it done in a way where Annemarie can survive, then that will be the canonical ending. Annemarie will be mentioned in passing during [[The Town||the second game]] and [[The Hunt (Project)|the third game]], regardless. If Annemarie can survive, she will appear in the third game to help Moe track down Priest.
