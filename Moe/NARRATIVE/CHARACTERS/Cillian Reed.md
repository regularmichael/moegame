Name subject to change

A victim who scrawls several notes Moe can find throughout her time there.

Note 1: Found in a room that isn't part of the standard floor plan, after the house changes in Chapter 1.
"My name is [Cillian Reed].

[[Victims|Sara]]'s gone. I'm the last one. I miss them. Sara, Adrian, Kristi.. was Thomas one? Did I make this up? Fuck. I need to write it all down. If I remind myself, I can't be forgotten. They keep finding my notes. I have to hide them better.

I saw her again, after camera lady got her. I ain't seen nothing like it. She was walking, I called out, nothing. No turn. Just ignored me. I cut her arm. She bled right. But no scream. What the fuck? She walked away like I ain't even there.

I ain't smart enough for this magic stuff or whatever is happening. I watch the lady sometimes, see she's drawing weird shit on the walls and the dirt and some little books. That's bad stuff really happens. I'll try to learn how she do it, that's what Adrian was trying to do before he was caught. Started practicing at night when I see her drag others into the field and I can't see them no more.

It's working. I made this room. I think. Hard to tell. But I feel safe here. I'll try more and more things. I have to get out of here. For Sara, for Adrian, for  (long break)  Krisi. And Thomas if he was one. Coming back if I find other names."

Additionally, a monster that can be defeated is Sara, either found through psychometry or by players looking at the scar on the forearm.
