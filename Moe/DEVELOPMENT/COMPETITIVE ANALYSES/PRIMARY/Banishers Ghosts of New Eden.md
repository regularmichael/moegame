**Brief Synopsis:**
Characters play Red mac Raith, a banisher, and Antea Duarte, his deceased lover, as they work to rid New Eden of a variety of courses. As they play the game, they can make choices on how to deal with their situations. This will impact the remainder of the game. The overarching choice of the game - to bring Antea back at the cost of human lives, or to allow her to ascend peacefully while leaving Red alone - is a major thread.

**CHARACTERS**
Players control both Red mac Raith and Antea Duarte, swapping between the two at will, although the game defaults to Red for most of the time. 

Red is idealistic and emotional, while Antea is reserved and pragmatic. Their dynamic as a couple is a primary point of the game, and the decision the player makes about major cases can shape how their relationship develops.

**EXPLORATION**
Exploration is interesting, and can be a useful prototype for Psychometry. Players are given items in the world, which provide clues or context for events in the world. As they explore, a red or blue sigil will flash on the players hand. A red sigil requires Red to conduct a ritual, while a blue sigil requires Antea to manipulate spiritual residue in the world. 

Red's rituals require items found in the world, and they are roughly broken up into 3 types: call a spirit, get a vision, or summon malevolent force. The spirit and vision elements specifically allow the player to interact with spectral recreations of events or characters in the world. This is an interesting, if basic idea, and I think we can use this as a template to expand on Moe's spellcasting abilities and Psychometric visions.

Antea, when needing to manipulate spiritual residue, will see lingering spiritual threads and gain insight into certain curses, hauntings, or other supernatural forces in the world. This is a good way to include Psychometry, we just need to make it more potent and also more dangerous. 

**COMBAT**
Players have a decent range of combat options. At first, players rely on melee attacks, although ranged options (such as rifles) appear to unlock later. Attacks generally fall into light/heavy attacks, each with a range of combos that can be unlocked as players progress in the game.

A unique thing about this game is the dual Red/Antea combat options. Players can swap between the two so long as Antea has enough spirit energy to use. Everything Antea does in combat requires spirit energy, which is generated by Red's regular attacks. Antea is best used against "possessed" enemies. There are many skills that require swapping between the two characters in order to be effective.
