## **PUZZLE INTEGRATION**

### **Glyph Lock Puzzles**

**Basic Structure:**

- Door/barrier has 2-3 glyph slots (visible or revealed by Chalk)
- Player must "charge" all glyphs to unlock
- Use rituals (passive charging) or manual focus

**Example:**

- Door requires Circle + Triangle
- Place Athame ritual (has Circle + Triangle slots)
- Wait for passive charging (25% per second = 4 seconds each)
- Or actively focus to speed up
- Both glyphs complete → Door unlocks

### **Multi-Altar Puzzles**

**Complex Structure:**

- 2-3 altars in same room
- Each requires different glyphs
- Must have all "charged" simultaneously
- Cannot stack rituals (one at a time)

**Solution Path:**

- Place ritual at Altar 1 (charge its glyphs)
- Dismiss, quickly place at Altar 2 (charge its glyphs)
- Altars slowly drain if not maintained (5-second grace)
- Must work quickly or use specific ritual items/consumables

**Example:**

- Altar 1: Circle + Triangle (Athame perfect)
- Altar 2: Square + Pentagon (Staff configured for this)
- Place Athame ritual at Altar 1 → charge → dismiss
- Quickly place Staff ritual at Altar 2 → charge → dismiss
- If both charged within grace window → Door opens
