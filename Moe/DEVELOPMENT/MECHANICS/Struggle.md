### **Struggle System (Optional Mechanic)**

#### **Overview**

Enemy grab/pin sequences requiring quick thinking to escape. Creates high-stakes moments with multiple solution paths.

---

#### **Trigger**

**Conditions:**

- Enemy performs grab attack (telegraphed, 1-second windup)
- Player fails to dodge
- Camera shifts to over-shoulder or first-person (close-up)
- Enemy pinning Moe down, choking, or restraining

**Prevention:**

- Dodge grab windup (preemptive avoidance)
- Stun enemy before grab completes (interrupt)
- Maintain distance (don't let enemies get close)

---

#### **Escape Options**

**Option A: Intuition QTE**

- 2-3 intuition prompts appear rapidly (0.5 sec each)
- Hit all prompts correctly: Clean escape, no damage
- Miss prompts: Take damage (10-20), harder escape

**Option B: Telekinetic Throw**

- Nearby object highlighted automatically
- Use telekinetic throw (10 mana)
- Object hits enemy, knockback, instant escape
- Take minor damage (10)

**Option C: Shockwave Panic**

- Use Bell shockwave (40 mana)
- Instant escape, enemy stunned
- No damage taken
- Expensive but reliable

**Option D: Button Mash (Fallback)**

- Mash button to fill escape bar
- Slowest option, takes 2-3 seconds
- Take damage over time (20-30 total)
- Always succeeds eventually (no fail state)

---

#### **Strategic Implications**

**Mana Economy:**

- Saved mana for escape? (Shockwave/Telekinetic)
- Or low mana, must use QTE or mashing?

**Preparation:**

- Using Bell ritual (slow zone) prevents grabs
- Positioning near objects enables telekinetic option

**Skill Expression:**

- Good players use intuition QTE (fastest, no damage)
- Panicked players use mashing (slow, costly)
- Prepared players never get grabbed (avoidance)

---

#### **Balancing Rules**

**Prevent Spam:**

- Enemy can only grab once per encounter
- After escape, 30-second cooldown before same enemy type can grab again
- Clear telegraph (1-second windup, audio cue, visual animation)

**Ensure Achievability:**

- At least one escape option always available
- Nearby object always present (for telekinetic)
- Intuition timing forgiving (not twitch-reflex)
- Button mash always succeeds (no instant death)

**Camera Clarity:**

- Smooth transition (not disorienting)
- Clear view of available options (highlighted objects)
- UI prompts obvious (what button to press?)
