## **4. CHALICE & CONSUMABLES**

### **Overview**

The Chalice is the catalyst for all ritual magic, and consumables are materials that can be added to the Chalice to enhance or modify ritual effects. Consumables add strategic depth by allowing players to adapt rituals to specific situations, but they are limited resources that must be found or crafted.

---

### **Chalice System**

#### **Role**

- Required anchor for all ritual placement
- Items cannot be deployed as rituals without Chalice
- Acts as "ritual caster" tool

#### **Properties**

- Always equipped/available (cannot be lost)
- No direct weapon attack
- Can accept consumables before lighting
- Visible as physical object when placed (glowing bowl/chalice)

#### **States**

**Unplaced:**

- In inventory, ready to deploy
- No effect, no visibility

**Placed (Unlit):**

- Physical object on ground
- Can be picked up and moved (instant, no penalty)
- Consumable can be added while unlit (instant from menu)
- No timer active, no commitment

**Lit (Active, No Item):**

- Chalice burning with flame
- Cannot be moved (committed to location)
- If consumable added: Timer begins countdown
- Waiting for item selection (player can choose when to deploy)

**Deployed (Full Ritual):**

- Chalice + chosen ritual item active
- Full zone effects in play
- Item removed from player's weapon inventory
- Cannot reposition (must dismiss entire ritual)

---

### **Consumable System**

#### **Overview**

Consumables are materials added to the Chalice before lighting to grant temporary bonuses or special effects to the ritual. They are finite resources that must be found in the world or crafted at safe rooms.

---

### **Consumable Types**

#### **Salt**

**Primary Effect:** Enhanced mana regeneration

**Properties:**

- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Effect:** +20% mana per second (total: 22/sec in ritual zone)
- **Use Case:** Sustained combat, resource recovery, long fights

**Visual:**

- Chalice glows brighter white
- Mana bar sparkles/shimmers when regenerating in zone

**Tactical Applications:**

- Place before prolonged encounter (know enemies are coming)
- Pair with defensive ritual (Bell slow) for safe recovery
- Enables aggressive play (spend mana freely, recover quickly)

---

#### **Chalk**

**Primary Effect:** Reveals hidden information

**Properties:**

- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Effect (Multi-Function):**
    1. Enemy glyphs visible instantly (no focus needed)
    2. Full glyph sequences revealed (see Circle → Triangle → Square all at once)
    3. Enemy footprints glow for 5 seconds after leaving zone (tracking)
    4. Interactive objects highlighted (glowing outline)

**Use Cases:**

- **Combat:** Fast enemy identification, skip learning phase
- **Stealth:** Track enemy patrols without line of sight
- **Puzzles:** Reveal hidden glyphs on doors/walls/objects
- **Exploration:** Find missable interactables

**Visual:**

- Chalice glows pale blue
- Zone has faint chalk dust particle effect
- Revealed elements glow white/blue

**Tactical Applications:**

- Enter unknown room → place Chalk ritual → scout safely
- Facing new enemy types → instant glyph knowledge
- Avoiding combat → track enemy movements, sneak past
- Puzzle-solving → reveal requirements without trial/error

---

#### **Herb Bundle**

**Primary Effect:** Extended mana lock grace period

**Properties:**

- **Duration:** 45 seconds
- **Effect:** Mana lock decay delay increased (5 seconds → 8 seconds)
- **Use Case:** Multi-enemy juggling, complex glyph sequences, chaotic fights

**Visual:**

- Chalice emits green smoke
- Locked glyphs have leafy particle effects

**Tactical Applications:**

- Lock multiple enemies' progress, longer window to switch targets
- Enables "stagger lock" strategy (lock → switch → return)
- Reduces pressure in overwhelming scenarios
- Rewards active target switching

---

#### **Incense**

**Primary Effect:** Temporary protective barrier

**Properties:**

- **Duration:** 15 seconds OR until broken (3-hit durability)
- **Effect:** Barrier blocks enemy movement into zone (invisible wall)
- **Use Case:** Emergency defense, setup protection, safe zone creation

**Visual:**

- Chalice trails white smoke
- Barrier edge visible as shimmering heat-wave effect
- Flashes when hit (durability indicator)

**Tactical Applications:**

- Place before vulnerable action (setting up Staff ritual)
- Block enemy rushes during critical moment
- Create safe space for mana recovery or healing
- Protect NPC during escort sections

**Breaking Conditions:**

- Takes 3 enemy hits (attacks/charges)
- Visual: Cracks appear on barrier after each hit
- Audio: Glass stress sound on hit, shatter sound on break

---

#### **Oil**

**Primary Effect:** Expanded ritual zone radius

## **Properties:**
**Duration:** Duration of ritual (permanent while ritual active)

- **Effect:** +50% ritual zone radius (8m → 12m, Staff 10m → 15m)
- **Use Case:** Large areas, covering more ground, affecting distant enemies

**Visual:**

- Chalice flame burns brighter/taller
- Ritual zone boundary more visible (stronger glow)
- Liquid sheen on ground within expanded zone

**Tactical Applications:**

- Cover multiple enemy spawn points simultaneously
- Defend large objective areas (escort paths, puzzle locations)
- Compensate for poor placement (wider zone = more forgiving positioning)
- Pair with Bell ritual (larger slow zone = better crowd control)

---

#### **Wax**

**Primary Effect:** Accelerated ritual setup

**Properties:**

- **Duration:** Next ritual only (one-time use)
- **Effect:** Ritual setup time reduced by 50%
    - Athame/Wand/Bell: 2 seconds → 1 second
    - Staff: 5 seconds → 2.5 seconds
- **Use Case:** Quick deployment, reactive placement, high-pressure situations

**Visual:**

- Chalice flame ignites faster (animation speed doubled)
- Item deployment more rapid (particles move faster)

**Tactical Applications:**

- React to surprise encounters (place ritual quickly)
- Staff becomes viable in chaotic situations (setup less risky)
- Emergency repositioning (dismiss old, place new faster)
- Pair with high-value rituals (Staff with correct glyphs)

**Rarity Note:**

- Found less commonly than other consumables
- Rewards preparation ("I'll save Wax for tough fights")

---

### **Consumable Acquisition**

#### **Finding in World**

**Raw Materials (Common):**

- Salt deposits, herb patches, chalk stones
- Found during exploration (environmental objects)
- Enemy drops (low % chance on kill)
- Respawn in safe rooms (limited quantity)

**Completed Consumables (Uncommon):**

- Pre-made bundles in chests, shelves
- Rewards for optional objectives
- Hidden in secret areas
- One-time pickups (do not respawn)

---

#### **Crafting System**

**Safe Room Crafting (Optimal):**

- Access crafting table/altar in safe rooms
- Full toolkit available
- **Output:** 2 consumables per recipe
- Example: Raw Salt + Purified Water = 2 Salt consumables
- Safe, no time pressure, efficient

**Field Crafting (Emergency):**

- Makeshift altars/worktables found in levels
- Limited tools, improvised conditions
- **Output:** 1 consumable per recipe
- Risky (enemies nearby, no safety)
- Convenient (don't need to backtrack to safe room)

**Recipe Examples:**

|Consumable|Raw Materials|Yield (Safe Room)|Yield (Field)|
|---|---|---|---|
|Salt|Raw Salt + Purified Water|2|1|
|Chalk|Chalk Stone + Binding Agent|2|1|
|Herb Bundle|Fresh Herbs × 3|2|1|
|Incense|Resin + Dried Flowers|2|1|
|Oil|Plant Oil + Essence|2|1|
|Wax|Beeswax + Candle Wick|2|1|

**Journal Integration:**

- Recipes always visible in player's journal (press button to open)
- Shows required materials: "Salt = Raw Salt + Purified Water"
- Shows effects: "Salt: Increases mana regeneration in ritual zones (30 sec)"
- No memorization required (quality-of-life feature)

---

### **Strategic Resource Management**

#### **Consumable Scarcity**

- Limited crafting materials (must explore to find)
- Finite uses per consumable (one use = consumed)
- Hoarding vs. using: Classic survival horror tension
- "Do I use Incense now or save for harder fight?"

#### **Loadout Planning**

- Limited inventory slots for consumables (e.g., 5 slots, each holds 3 of one type)
- Must choose which to bring from safe room
- Example loadout: 3 Salt, 2 Chalk, 1 Incense, 1 Wax
- Wrong loadout = harder fights (but not impossible)

#### **Consumable Synergies**

- **Salt + Wand:** High mana regen + heavy damage focus = aggressive sustained offense
- **Chalk + Staff:** Reveal glyphs → configure Staff correctly = optimal setup
- **Incense + Bell:** Barrier + slow = impenetrable defense zone
- **Herb + Athame:** Extended lock grace + fixed Circle/Triangle slots = easy multi-enemy juggling
- **Oil + Bell:** Large slow zone = control entire battlefield

---

### **Chalice Timing Strategy**

#### **Consumable Timer Mechanics**

**Timer Start Condition:**

- Consumable duration begins when Chalice is **lit** (not when placed)
- Lit but no item deployed yet: Timer ticking (creates urgency)
- Item deployed: Timer continues from lit moment

**Strategic Implications:**

**Light First (Reactive):**

- **Pros:** See enemy glyphs → choose appropriate item → deploy
- **Cons:** Consumable timer starts immediately → pressure to choose fast
- **Use When:** Uncertain which item is best, need to observe first

**Item First (Planned):**

- **Pros:** No timer pressure → choose consumable → light when ready
- **Cons:** Committed to item choice before lighting (can't change without dismissing)
- **Use When:** Know enemy types, have specific strategy in mind

**Example Scenario (Salt, 30-second timer):**

1. Place Chalice + add Salt (timer NOT active yet)
2. Light Chalice (timer starts: 30... 29... 28...)
3. Enemy reveals Square glyph
4. Choose Wand (has 1 customizable slot, set to Square)
5. Wand deploys (took 3 seconds total)
6. Salt still active for 27 seconds

**Wasted Consumable Risk:**

- Light Chalice with Salt → 5 seconds pass → choose wrong item → dismiss ritual
- Salt already consumed 5 seconds of duration
- Must replace ritual quickly or waste Salt entirely
- Teaches: Plan before lighting, or accept waste
