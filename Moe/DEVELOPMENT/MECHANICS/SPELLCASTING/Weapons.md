### **Overview**

Ritual items serve dual purposes: they function as weapons for direct combat AND can be placed as rituals to create persistent magical zones. Players must choose between using an item as a weapon (immediate tactical option) or deploying it as a ritual (strategic area control at the cost of losing that weapon).

---

### **Ritual Items (Weapons)**

#### **Athame**

**Role:** Light/fast weapon, reliable starter tool

**Weapon Properties:**

- **Attack:** Light Bolt
- **Mana Cost:** 10
- **Cast Time:** 0.5 seconds
- **Damage:** Small (20)
- **Effect:** 1-second stun
- **Visual:** Quick blue projectile
- **Audio:** Sharp "ping" sound

**Ritual Properties:**

- **Setup Time:** 2 seconds (after Chalice lit)
- **Zone Size:** 8-meter diameter
- **Passive Glyph Slots:** 2 (Circle + Triangle, fixed/non-customizable)
- **Passive Charge Rate:** 25% per second when matching enemy in zone
- **Active Focus Boost:** 50% faster charging when player focuses on enemy in zone
- **Special Effect:** None

**Tactical Identity:**

- "The Sidearm" - Always useful, quick response
- Best for: Common enemy types (Circle/Triangle encounters)
- Weakness: Fixed slots can't adapt to unusual glyphs

**When to Use:**

- **As Weapon:** Fast poke damage, cheap mana cost, maintaining pressure
- **As Ritual:** Multi-enemy scenarios with Circle/Triangle glyphs, sustained combat

---

#### **Wand**

**Role:** Heavy/precision weapon, single-target specialist

**Weapon Properties:**

- **Attack:** Heavy Bolt
- **Mana Cost:** 25
- **Cast Time:** 1.2 seconds (longer = vulnerability window)
- **Damage:** Large (50)
- **Effect:** 2.5-second stun
- **Visual:** Large purple projectile with trail
- **Audio:** Deep "thrum" sound

**Ritual Properties:**

- **Setup Time:** 2 seconds
- **Zone Size:** 8-meter diameter
- **Passive Glyph Slots:** 1 (customizable - player chooses glyph)
- **Passive Charge Rate:** 25% per second
- **Active Focus Boost:** 75% faster charging (highest boost of all items)
- **Special Effect:** None

**Tactical Identity:**

- "The Sniper Rifle" - High impact, deliberate use
- Best for: Boss fights, single tough enemies, burst damage
- Weakness: Long cast time leaves player vulnerable, only 1 passive slot

**When to Use:**

- **As Weapon:** Finishing blows, stunning dangerous enemies, fighting from range
- **As Ritual:** Solo boss encounters where maximum focus boost matters, single-glyph scenarios

---

#### **Bell**

**Role:** AOE/crowd control, panic button

**Weapon Properties:**

- **Attack:** AOE Shockwave
- **Mana Cost:** 40 (expensive)
- **Cast Time:** 1 second (windup animation)
- **Damage:** Medium (30)
- **Effect:** 360° knockback (5 meters) + 2.5-second stun to all enemies within range
- **Range:** 6-meter radius around player
- **Visual:** Expanding golden ring from Moe
- **Audio:** Deep resonant bell toll

**Ritual Properties:**

- **Setup Time:** 2 seconds
- **Zone Size:** 8-meter diameter
- **Passive Glyph Slots:** 0 (none)
- **Passive Charge Rate:** N/A (no glyph charging)
- **Active Focus Boost:** 50% (standard)
- **Special Effect:** All enemies in zone slowed by 30%

**Tactical Identity:**

- "The Shotgun" - Close-range defense, area denial
- Best for: Being surrounded, creating space, emergency escape
- Weakness: High mana cost, no passive glyph support

**When to Use:**

- **As Weapon:** Surrounded by enemies, need immediate breathing room, breaking enemy rushes
- **As Ritual:** Sustained encounters where slowing enemies provides tactical advantage, protecting specific areas

---

#### **Staff**

**Role:** Melee/defensive weapon, ultimate tactical tool

**Weapon Properties:**

- **Attack:** Magical Melee Strike
- **Mana Cost:** 15
- **Cast Time:** 1.5 seconds (slow swing)
- **Damage:** Medium (40)
- **Effect:** Heavy knockback (8 meters)
- **Range:** Close (2 meters - must be near enemy)
- **Visual:** Staff glows, sweeping arc animation
- **Audio:** Whoosh + impact thud

**Ritual Properties:**

- **Setup Time:** 5 seconds (longest/most vulnerable)
- **Zone Size:** 10-meter diameter (largest)
- **Passive Glyph Slots:** 4 (fully customizable - player configures all)
- **Passive Charge Rate:** 33% per second (fastest passive rate)
- **Active Focus Boost:** 50% (standard)
- **Special Effect:** None

**Tactical Identity:**

- "The Tactician's Gambit" - High ceiling, high floor
- Best for: Known encounters (boss rematches), prepared situations, multi-glyph complexity
- Weakness: Long setup is vulnerable, wrong glyph loadout makes it useless, late-game acquisition

**When to Use:**

- **As Weapon:** Defensive knockback when cornered, melee range defense
- **As Ritual:** Pre-planned encounters where you know enemy glyphs, complex multi-enemy scenarios, situations where 5-second setup is safe

**Configuration Note:**

- Requires pre-placement glyph selection menu
- Can load any combination of learned glyphs (Circle, Circle, Triangle, Square or Circle, Triangle, Hexagon, Pentagon, etc.)
- Wrong configuration severely impacts effectiveness

### **Telekinetic Throw**

**Role:** Always-available environmental weapon

**Properties:**

- **Type:** Innate ability (not an item, always accessible)
- **Mana Cost:** 10
- **Cast Time:** Instant (grab + throw)
- **Damage:** Variable based on object size
    - Small objects (bottle, rock): 10 damage
    - Medium objects (chair, tool): 20 damage
    - Large objects (crate, furniture): 30 damage
- **Effect:** Brief stun on impact
- **Range:** 15-meter grab range, 20-meter throw range

**Tactical Identity:**

- Environmental interaction tool
- Cheap damage option
- Utility for puzzles (moving objects to reveal paths)

**Usage Notes:**

- Requires grabbable objects in environment (level design consideration)
- Objects highlighted when nearby (subtle glow/outline)
- Objects are consumed after throw (can't reuse broken bottle)
- Can be used regardless of equipped weapon
- Some objects have special properties (oil lantern = fire damage, mirror shard = confusion)

### **Weapon Loadout**

**Carrying Capacity:**

- Chalice (always equipped, cannot be removed)
- Athame (always available)
- Wand (always available)
- Bell (always available)
- Staff (always available)
- Telekinetic Throw (innate ability, always accessible)

**Total:** 4 ritual items + Chalice + telekinetic ability

**Weapon Swapping:**

- Radial menu (hold button, time slows 50%)
- Select weapon, release button
- 0.5-second equip animation (vulnerable during swap)
- Cannot swap while attacking (must complete current action)

**Design Philosophy:**

- All weapons available (similar to Silent Hill)
- Scarcity comes from mana cost and ritual commitment, not inventory limits
- Encourages experimentation without punishment
