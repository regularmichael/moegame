With the exception of the Prologue and Chapter 10, players can find a living a creature that they can save. Doing so contributes to the Living Things quest. Characters will not be able to return to free the living thing if they progress too far into the game.

*Quest Start*
Chapter 1 - Break and Enter. 

*Completion Criteria*
- Free all Living Things from the clutches of the farm
- Do not use Living Things targets for spells
- Do not outright kill Living Things
- Protect Living Things from enemies, if necessary.

NOTE: By default, players who beat the game will always have 1 / 10, as Midnight leaving with Moe during the the final chapter counts as a living thing saved.

*REWARD:*
- Achievement
- Alternate Outfit - beekeeper? jumpsuit?
- Mechanical reward, i.e. bonus resources on NG+?

*Sidequest Breakdown*
Prologue: None.

Chapter 1 - Farmhouse Floors 1 and 2: A crow will be flying around a room on the second floor. Opening a window near it will cause it to fly out, fulfilling this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The crow will be warped upon later visits to the second floor. Players can release it like an enemy.
~~~

Chapter 2 - The Yard and the Garage: A spider has made a web in the broken remnants of a recording camera. Moe can scoop it into her hand or a jar, then release it at the next save point, fulfilling this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The spider and its web are gone. 

NOTE: The spider is a possible solution to the [[Forced Warden Puzzle]]. Using the spider as part of this solution will kill it, meaning this animal will not be saved.
~~~

Chapter 3 - The Barn: As players explore the twisting approach to the barn, they will find a cow calf being attacked by a [[Barn Abomination]] tendril. They can save the calf, which will then run past Moe and fulfill this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The calf will be dragged into the darkness by the tendril. It can be found as part of the Barn Abomination later.
~~~


Chapter 4 - Farmhouse Floors 3 and 4: When players come across the McCall child's room on the third floor, they will see a pet rabbit in a cage, well cared for as though someone regularly tends to it. Players can take the cage with them to a save point and release it, fulfilling this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The rabbit will be dead when you return to lay the remains of the McCall child in his bed.
~~~


Chapter 5 - The Basement and The Grain Silo: While in the basement of the McCall house, during the scene where Moe must avoid a large enemy, a small pack of rats can be found trying to scratch their way out of a locked door that leads to the outside. Keeping them alive during this sequence will see them flee when the door opens, fulfilling this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The only way the rats will not be freed is if they are actively used as a distraction against the large enemy or if Moe fails a puzzle attempt near them. Doing any of these will kill the rats.
~~~


Chapter 6 - The Creek: Before Moe conduct her water-reading ritual and dives into the waterworld of the creek to retrieve the McCall child remains, she'll see a turtle stuck on its back. She can either flip it over, giving a 50% chance to survive as it crawls away from the creek (or 100% if Waylon is alive). Otherwise, she can hold onto the turtle in a box while she completes the chapter, then release it when she is purged from the Ridgewreath Farm magical pocket.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: She will never see the turtle again, as we don't revisit the creek.

Flipping the turtle over while having a friendly Waylon will ensure the turtle survives, as he watches her care for the animal, which sparks some of his humanity.

If Moe chooses to keep the turtle on her person, she'll need to avoid damage or taking too long before she releases it, otherwise it will die. The first chance she'll have to release it is immediately after the creek, but savvy players who are quick and careful can realistically carry the turtle with them to the end of the game. This is a good achievement!
~~~


Chapter 7 - Return to the House: During the Osenists' disastrous attempts to get the house under control, Moe may come across a young Osenist cowering in a closet, traumatized by what he is seeing. Moe can opt to bring him along with her and protect him. If he makes it out of the house with Moe, he will flee the Ridgewreath Farm magical pocket, fulfilling this requirement. 
~~~
IF NOT FREED: If found but left behind (either "i'll come back later" or "you're on your own" options), he will die at the hands of the Mandragora. Otherwise, he will not appear for the rest of the game. 
~~~


Chapter 8 - The Yard: During the mad dash to the "strange house" in the distance, players may see a possum playing dead. Poking it several times will cause it to flee, fulfilling this requirement.
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The possum will be sucked dry of its life force by a warped Osenist. 
~~~

Chapter 9 - Emilio's House: Moe can find a potted flower that the twins were going to give to Cassie. Moe can carry it with her then leave it in Bessie when she reaches a save point, fulfilling this requirement. 
~~~
IF NOT FREED: The flower will wilt as the Mandragora appears behind Moe to chase her.

NOTE: The flower is a possible key item used to progress in the house. If Moe uses it, it will wither, meaning it will not be saved.
~~~


Chapter 10 - Ritual Site: Midnight will always survive the final fight, and thus will always be at least 1 "Living Thing" saved as shown in the results screen.
