### **1. Resourcefulness**

- Limited mana, consumables, tools
- Multiple solutions to problems (combat, stealth, puzzles)
- Rewards clever use of environment (telekinetic throw)
- No "correct" approach, just efficient vs. inefficient

### **2. Knowledge as Power**

- Learning enemy glyphs = mastery (faster subsequent encounters)
- Psychometry reveals secrets (but attracts danger)
- Understanding systems = survival (rituals, mana lock, consumables)
- Detective identity: Observation and deduction are tools

### **3. Meaningful Choices**

- Kill vs. release (materials vs. mana)
- Weapon vs. ritual (immediate vs. strategic)
- Consumable usage (use now vs. save for later)
- Mana lock (preserve progress vs. maintain flexibility)
- Psychometry risk (knowledge vs. Mandragora danger)

### **4. Tension Without Frustration**

- Mandragora is scary but escapable (not instant death)
- Mana lock prevents total loss (grace periods)
- Rituals can be repositioned (if unlit)
- Multiple escape options (distance, hiding, safe rooms)
- Difficulty comes from decisions, not execution barriers

### **5. Systems Interconnection**

- Rituals use weapons (lose attack option)
- Psychometry attracts Mandragora (investigation has cost)
- Mana lock enables multi-enemy combat (but shrinks resources)
- Consumables enhance rituals (tactical depth)
- Every system affects others (no isolated mechanics)
