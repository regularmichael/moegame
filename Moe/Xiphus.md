Xiphus is a magic lantern that becomes [[Moe|Moe's]] [[Haze Token]] in the second game.

Xiphus is a legendary Token that has several appearances throughout the recorded history of magic. Its origin is lost to time, but it is well known (and sometimes feared) for its ability to uncover the truth. The exact mechanism of how it works is unknown, but the wielders of Xiphus almost instinctively know how to use it the minute it appears to them. Thus, a common belief is that it has a mind of its own, using the wielders as tool just as much as they use Xiphus.

Xiphus, like other Tokens, is usable only in the haze. It emits a silver light, whose rays' edges suggest a blue fog present at all times. The light can clarify some of the fuzzier images in the Haze, but it can also be used to blind or distract Haze-based entities such as [[Mandragora (Narrative)|the Mandragora]]. It will prove to be instrumental in Moe's opposition to [[Priest(Narrative)|Priest]] and freeing [[The Town (Project)|the town]] from his control.

Moe will first use Xiphus during the tutorial of the second game, when she has her prophetic dream about the events of that game. She will find herself using it as though she has always had it. Upon waking up, she will be holding it in her hands, before it fades back into the haze. She will be alarmed that a Haze Token has chosen her, remarking that it is an ominous portent of what is to come. 

Over the course of the game, she will have the opportunity to explore more of Xiphus' history and true origin.
