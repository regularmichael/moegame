*Scenario 1*

After unlocking a door with a key, Moe finds herself in need of a spell to lift of a magic barrier that still holds it shut. On examining the barrier with Psychometry, she discovers that it is being bound with 3 glyphs - circle, triangle, and square.

Consulting her journal, she learns that she already knows the ritual of unlocking needed to remove this barrier. It requires creating a magical area that includes the door. She also knows that she has 4 glyphs known already: her personal glyph, circle, triangle, and octagon. She does not possess the square, so she has to find it.

In her explorations, she finds that a book is recently removed from a bookcase. The bookcase is strongly radiating a magical energy, so there has to be something important. Moe removes her glove and touches the empty spot on the bookcase. She sees a vision of the book coming off of the shelf by an unknown hand and taken to the parlor. 

Arriving at the parlor, Moe spots a book suspiciously out of place. She removes her glove again to see that it was the book removed from the shelf. Flipping through its pages, she sees that there are hastily scribbled notes. She touches it psychometrically again to revisit the moment these notes were written, and in doing so she learns the glyph needed to remove the barrier. It is added to her inventory of glyphs permanently.

Returning to the door, Moe sets the ritual in motion - she selects her ritual focus item, which creates the magical area. She then inlays the glyphs in their correct position according to the ritual, then expends mana to complete the ritual. The barrier fades, allowing her to progress.

*Scenario 2*

After felling the magical barrier, Moe tries the door once again. It doesn't budge - despite the key unlocking the door and the barrier being dispelled, there is something blocking the door behind it. She can only open it a little. Determining that she must instead find another way into the room, probably through an outside window, she turns to leave. After taking a step away, a monster crashes through the door, stumbling over the crates and other debris that was blocking entrance.

Moe has never seen this enemy before. She places a few attacks on it, slowing it down slightly so she can buy herself some time to learn the monster's glyphs. As she focuses on it, she learns that she can use the circle glyph on it. Focusing on the circle glyph, she focuses her magical energy on it. The glyph begins to fill up, but the monster attacks her while she focuses.

The monster corners her. She breaks glyph focus to attack it again, this time with a short magical burst that stuns it and allows her to push back. Refocusing, she sees that she has lost focus on the glyph. She starts to fill it up again.

The glyph changes to the square glyph, and it starts to drain once again. Moe refocuses with the square glyph equipped, and the glyph begins to fill up. Now aware of her surroundings, she is able to move around the monster's attacks while she focuses.

One the glyph is filled, Moe crushes a ball of energy in her hand, which locks the enemy in place and regains some of her mana. 

*Scenario 2 Outcome 1:*
Moe continues to attack the monster with gunfire, now able to land the killing blow on it since the magical protection on it is suspended. It dies, leaving her some material components.

*Scenario 2 Outcome 2:*
Moe now finds herself in a position to release the monster from its servitude. She touches the monster to see its history and learns that an item nearby is special to it - a locket it wore when it was still human. She runs over to grab it, then returns to the monster to release it. It peacefully dissolves, leaving nothing behind but fully replenishing Moe's mana.

*Scenario 3*
In an open field, Moe is beset by 2 monsters similar to the one described in Scenario 2. As she focuses, she learns that one requires the circle glyph, while the other requires the octagon glyph. She hits one with a magical shockwave to stun it, then she decides to use a ritual, much like the one described in scenario one. This is a basic ritual shape with 3 slots that she can draw, one in an area on the ground. 

She casts her ritual item onto the ground, which sets up the ritual area on her behalf. This leaves her vulnerable, so she makes sure she has enough space to make the ritual. She loads one slot with the circle glyph, and the other with the octagon glyph. When the enemies step into the ritual area, their glyphs start filling up - respective to the circle and octagon glyphs they are displaying.

Combat plays like described in Scenario 2. Moe chooses to focus with the circle glyph, which makes that glyph fill up faster than the octagonal glyph due to 2 sources of focus on that glyph. It fills, and Moe stuns the monster. However, the octagonal-glyphed monster is still pursuing her. It also has changes to a square glyph, so its progression continues to fall. She shoots it a couple of times, causing it to stumble back so she can perform the releasing ritual on the stunned monster. 

When she returns focus to the other monster, its glyph progression has fallen entirely, and it is outside of the ritual zone. As a result of its wounds, it moves slowly. Moe tries to complete its glyph sequence, but it dies before she can, and thus cannot release it.
